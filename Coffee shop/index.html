Got Questions?

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about our beans, café rules, and catering.

Do you offer dairy-free and vegan milk options?

▼
Yes! We offer organic oat milk, almond milk, and coconut milk at no additional charge for any espresso beverage.

Where do you source your coffee beans?

▼
We work directly with certified organic fair-trade farms in Ethiopia, Colombia, Guatemala, and Sumatra. Every batch is traceable to the farm origin.

Can I buy coffee beans for brewing at home?

▼
Absolutely! We sell 250g and 1kg bags of whole beans or custom-ground coffee in-store and online with free local delivery.

Is there high-speed WiFi and space for laptop work?

▼
Yes! We welcome remote workers and students. We offer fast 500Mbps WiFi and dedicated seating areas equipped with power sockets.

Do you host private events or coffee workshops?

▼
We host weekend latte art classes and barista tasting workshops! Contact our team via the form below to inquire about private venue rentals.

Our Heritage

PASSIONATE ABOUT EVERY BEAN WE ROAST.

Founded in 2016, Enaco Craft began as a humble roastery with a singular mission: to bring ethically sourced, small-batch coffee to our community. We partner directly with sustainable farmers from Colombia, Ethiopia, and Guatemala.

Every morning, our master baristas carefully adjust grinder settings and extraction parameters to highlight the unique floral, fruity, and chocolate notes of each roast profile.
